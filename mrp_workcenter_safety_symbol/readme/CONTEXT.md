In manufacturing settings, safety procedures are often tied to specific locations, machines, or workstations (represented as "Work Centers" in Odoo MRP). There is a business need to clearly associate standard safety symbols (like required PPE or warnings) with the relevant Work Centers. This allows for:

* Quick visual reference for operators and supervisors.
* Inclusion of relevant symbols in documentation like work instructions or travelers.
* Foundation for safety checks or compliance tracking related to specific equipment.

*Example Use Case:* A metal press Work Center always requires "Wear Eye Protection" (M004) and "Wear Hearing Protection" (M003). This module allows linking these two symbols directly to the press's Work Center record in Odoo.
