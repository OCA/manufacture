To associate safety symbols with a Work Center:

1.  Ensure you have installed the necessary `base_iso7010_data_*` modules to load the symbols you want to use.
2.  Navigate to the Manufacturing app.
3.  Go to Configuration > Work Centers.
4.  Open the Work Center record you wish to configure or create a new one.
5.  Click on the "Safety Symbols" tab.
6.  You will see an area displaying currently selected symbols (initially empty) as small visual cards (Kanban view).
7.  Click "Add a line" (or similar text/button that appears). A modal window will appear.
8.  Select one or more safety symbols from the list. You can use the search bar to find symbols by name or code.
9.  Click "Select".
10. The selected symbols will appear as cards on the "Safety Symbols" tab.
11. To remove a symbol, you may need to open the selection modal again or use controls on the Kanban card.
12. Click "Save" on the Work Center form.

The selected symbols are now associated with this Work Center. This module itself does not perform further actions based on these symbols.
