Go to *Manufacturing \> Operations \> Manufacturing Orders*, open an
order whose bill of materials has operations and check the
**Sequence** column in its **Work Orders** tab. Work orders are numbered
1, 2, 3... in the order they are displayed, and work orders added
manually get the next number. When work orders are removed, reordered
or regenerated (e.g. by changing the bill of materials), the remaining
ones are renumbered from 1 without gaps, keeping their order.
