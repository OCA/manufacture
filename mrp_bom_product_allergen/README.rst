This module enhances the Bill of Materials (BoM) structure report by
adding allergen information. It also introduces the ability to
automatically compute a product’s allergens based on its BoM.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

This module does not require any additional configuration.

Usage
=====

To print the Bill of Materials (BoM) structure report:

1. Open **Any Bill of Materials**
2. Go to **BoM Overview**
3. Click **Print**

To compute product allergens based on their BoMs:

1. Locate the **Compute Allergens from BoM** button under the
   *Allergens* field.
2. The same action can also be triggered from the list view.
