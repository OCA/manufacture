In a Make To Order flow, the finished move of a manufacturing order
feeds the ensuing moves (e.g. the delivery). By default, if the
manufacturing order produces a different quantity than the planned one,
the demand of these ensuing moves is not updated.

This module pushes the change of quantity produced to the demand of the
ensuing moves, along the whole chain of destination moves:

- Producing more than planned increases the demand.
- Producing less than planned without creating a backorder decreases the
  demand.
- Producing less than planned and creating a backorder for the remaining
  quantity keeps the demand unchanged, as the backorder will still
  produce it.
- Cancelling a backorder manufacturing order decreases the demand by its
  remaining quantity.

Moves whose demand is reduced to zero are cancelled. Component moves of
another manufacturing order are never updated.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Operation Types*.
2. Open the *Manufacturing* operation type of the warehouse.
3. Check *Push Produced Quantity to MTO Moves*.

The option is disabled by default and only applies to the manufacturing
orders using this operation type.

Usage
=====

1. Enable *Push Produced Quantity to MTO Moves* on the *Manufacturing*
   operation type (see Configuration).
2. Configure a product with the *Manufacture* and *Replenish on Order
   (MTO)* routes.
3. Create a demand for 10 units (e.g. a delivery), which creates a
   manufacturing order for 10 units.
4. Produce 8 units and mark the manufacturing order as done without
   creating a backorder.
5. The demand of the delivery is now 8 units.
