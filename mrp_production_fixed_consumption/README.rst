In some processes the components are issued for the planned quantity of
a manufacturing order and the yield is not exact: the order plans 1,000
units, the material for 1,000 units is consumed, but only 998 good units
come out.

When the produced quantity is registered on the manufacturing order,
Odoo scales the consumed quantity of every component down to the
produced quantity, so the material lost in the process stays in stock
and the user has to correct the consumption of each component by hand.
When the bill of materials is set to block or warn about consumption
differences, Odoo also compares the consumption with the quantity scaled
by the produced quantity.

This module adds a *Fixed Consumption* option to the bill of materials.
On the manufacturing orders of such a bill, registering a produced
quantity keeps the consumption of the components at the planned
quantity, and the consumption check compares with the planned quantity
as well. Components whose consumption was registered by hand are kept as
they are, as in the standard flow.

Configuration
=============

On the bill of materials, next to the *Consumption* setting, tick *Fixed
Consumption*.

Usage
=====

Confirm a manufacturing order of a bill of materials with *Fixed
Consumption*, register a produced quantity lower than the planned one
and mark it as done without a backorder: the finished product is
received for the produced quantity and the components are consumed for
the planned quantity.

The option is meant for orders closed without a backorder. If a
backorder is created, the components are consumed in full on the first
order and the backorder has nothing left to consume.
