Confirm a manufacturing order of a bill of materials with *Fixed
Consumption*, register a produced quantity lower than the planned one and
mark it as done without a backorder: the finished product is received for
the produced quantity and the components are consumed for the planned
quantity.

The option is meant for orders closed without a backorder. If a backorder is
created, the components are consumed in full on the first order and the
backorder has nothing left to consume.
