Shows the Scheduled End Date on Manufacturing Orders, and allows to
change the start date from it, through an onchange.

It's useful for companies wanting to plan their manufacturing orders
from the target finish date. Usually these companies have long
manufacturing times (days or weeks).

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Manufacturing > Products > Bills of Materials* and set the
   *Manuf. Lead Time* of the BoM you want to plan from the finish date.
2. Go to *Manufacturing > Operations > Manufacturing Orders* and create
   a new manufacturing order.
3. Set the *Scheduled End* date to the desired finish date.
4. The *Scheduled Date* (start) is automatically set to *Scheduled End*
   minus the BoM's *Manuf. Lead Time*, and the dates of the raw material
   moves are updated accordingly.
