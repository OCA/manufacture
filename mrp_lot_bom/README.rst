When the same product can be manufactured with several Bills of
Materials, the units in stock cannot be told apart once the
manufacturing orders are done.

This module stores on each lot/serial number the Bill of Materials it
was manufactured with, and shows it in the inventory, where it can be
used to search and to group.

If a lot is produced by several manufacturing orders, the last one done
is the one kept.

Usage
=====

1. Manufacture a product tracked by lots or serial numbers, choosing the
   Bill of Materials on the manufacturing order.
2. Once the order is done, the lot/serial number shows the *Bill of
   Materials* in its form.
3. Go to *Inventory > Reporting > Stock*: the column *Bill of Materials*
   is next to *Lot/Serial Number*. You can search by it, or group with
   *Group By > Bill of Materials*.
4. The same column, search and grouping are in *Inventory > Products >
   Lots/Serial Numbers*.

Lots and serial numbers that already existed when the module is
installed get their values from the manufacturing orders already done.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- A lot/serial number keeps a single Bill of Materials: the one of the
  last done manufacturing order that produced it. For serial numbers
  this is exact, as a unit comes from a single manufacturing order. A
  lot, however, can be produced by several manufacturing orders with
  different Bills of Materials; in that case only the last one is shown
  and the previous ones are not visible on the lot nor on the inventory.
  The module could be extended to keep all the Bills of Materials used
  for a lot.
