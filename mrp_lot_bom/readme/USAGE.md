1. Manufacture a product tracked by lots or serial numbers, choosing the
   Bill of Materials on the manufacturing order.
2. Once the order is done, the lot/serial number shows the *Bill of
   Materials* in its form.
3. Go to *Inventory \> Reporting \> Stock*: the column *Bill of Materials*
   is next to *Lot/Serial Number*. You can search by it, or group with
   *Group By \> Bill of Materials*.
4. The same column, search and grouping are in *Inventory \> Products \>
   Lots/Serial Numbers*.

Lots and serial numbers that already existed when the module is installed
get their values from the manufacturing orders already done.
